penalty #1**, not Szoboszlai, so no spot-kick premium is assumed. The role change is a shift
in *how* he scores, not a cut to whether he does.

### Bench Boost in GW1: a genuinely divided elite question

New for 2026/27 (BB and TC are playable in GW1; WC/FH open at GW2), and the community has
split rather than converged:

- **For BB1:** Dan Bennett (10× top-10k) has a draft with **no Haaland**, spreading the
  budget evenly across all 15.
- **For BB2:** Copenhagen Wednesday (6× top-10k) is building toward Gameweek 2, targeting
  Coventry at home to Hull.
- **Against both:** GW1 carries too much uncertainty — new managers at nine clubs, three
  unreadable promoted sides, unestablished roles.
- @LetsTalk_FPL is holding the chip.

**Our no-chip call sits with the majority and is structurally consistent:** a £16.5m
minimum-viable bench and a Bench Boost are mutually exclusive strategies, and we chose the
former deliberately. No change.

**But one elite trend is a genuine critique of our squad, and it is not dismissable.**
Managers with 80+ top-10k finishes between them are carrying **two *playing* goalkeepers**,
so a Bench Boost can be fired later without first spending a free transfer to acquire a
playing No.2. With chips **doubled in 2026/27 (two of each, eight total)**, that flexibility
is worth materially more than it was last season — and our Dubravka is a deliberate
non-playing £4.0m. This does **not** change the GW1 squad (the £2.0m upgrade to a playing
No.2 would have to come out of the XI, which is the same failed trade as the Thiago package).
**Logged for `/fpl-chip-strategy`:** when the first BB window is planned, price in the
transfer cost of a playing second keeper, and consider making that the GW2–4 keeper move.

## Fixture / chip watch — and a dated-data trap to avoid

**@BenCrellin is the fixture-planning authority** and his 2026/27 blank/double calendar
lives at `fantasyfootballhub.co.uk/crellin-fpl-calendar`.

**Trap, flagged loudly:** the DGW/BGW posts that surface most readily in search — "Palace and
Man City will definitely Blank in Gameweek 31 (along with Arsenal and **Wolves**)", "Double
Gameweek 33 and Blank Gameweek 34 are now set … **Burnley** … Leeds vs **Wolves**" — are
**2025/26 artifacts**. Wolves and Burnley were relegated and are not in the 2026/27 league;
these also match the GW26/GW31 markers in our own *backtest* record, which is what they
describe. **Do not plan 2026/27 chips against them.**

**No 2026/27 blank or double gameweek is confirmed this early** — cup draws and European
qualification haven't produced the fixture collisions that create them. `/fpl-chip-strategy`
does **not** need re-running on this sweep. All 8 chips retained.
