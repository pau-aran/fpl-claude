Rolled the FT (banked 2 for GW12), locked Saliba, captained Haaland. No move.

- **Roll the FT — PROCESS PASS.** The squad was fit with 11 playing starters

  for the GW12 Liverpool-turn window. In hindsight the whole squad blanked, so
  no single FT move rescues a 34 — the disciplined bank was correct regardless

  (benched, 1) and Timber (1, Arsenal no CS) scored identically; the free clone
  swap would have netted exactly zero while burning the FT we banked. Seven

- **Hold the Liverpool bloc (Konaté + Szoboszlai) the crowd was selling —
  correct process, negative this week (−3 combined vs prediction).** Both
  blanked at City exactly as the "worst fixture of the run" read said. But
  selling a just-bought Konaté into one bad away fixture — one week before his
  run turns (NFO(H), WHU(A), SUN(H)), which is precisely when the community
  said to buy Liverpool BACK — would have been textbook churn. The one-week
  hold cost is the logged, accepted EO risk; GW12 judges it.
- **Captain Haaland — rule adherence 11/11.** Model top (7.38), ~90% EO, and
  the read was *right*: he scored in a 3-0 win. The missed penalty is
  unforecastable variance, not a bad call. Still the correct shield.
