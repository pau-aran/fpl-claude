## 2. The decision — graded

**ROLL to the 5-FT cap — PASS, and the reasoning is the most valuable thing in this
window.** The solver proposed a four-move Arsenal teardown (+7.53) driven by BGW31 sitting
inside its horizon. The memo counted the actual cost of the blank — **eleven of our fifteen
play in GW31, so the best legal XI is ten starters and the blank costs exactly one slot,
worth ~4 points, fixable with one free transfer**. Paying four transfers to pre-solve a
one-transfer problem five weeks out, while two of the proposed buys blank in GW31
themselves, was correctly refused. That count is what let GW28 close the whole problem in a
single move and keep both remaining chips.

**Captain Haaland — PASS.** 6.39 top of board, 0.55 clear, returned 6. First week our
captain differed from the community's (Scout went Palmer) and holding on our own numbers
was right.

## 3. [PROCESS FAIL] The bench-hair lever: reasoning written, action not taken

The memo's bench-hair section asked *"should Calvert-Lewin start?"* and answered, in
writing: **"the answer is no"** — he was coming off a zero-minute illness week and Leeds
were away at Chelsea, against three nailed 90-minute alternatives. Then the decision JSON
was submitted with **no `start`/`bench` override**. Calvert-Lewin started and scored **2**,
while **Casemiro (5) and Konaté (9) sat on the bench**. Cost: **−3 against the named
alternative, −7 against the best one.**

This is the GW19 failure repeated verbatim — *"the `force_start` lever existed and was NOT
used"* — by the manager who wrote the rule extension two weeks earlier and shipped the
tooling that surfaced the hair. The mechanical listing did its job perfectly: it printed
three margins, all naming Calvert-Lewin as the displaced player. **The gap is no longer
detection. It is that a written conclusion and an encoded decision are two different
artifacts, and only one of them is executed.**

> **[NEW RULE — GW30+] Every CALL OWED row must resolve to an explicit token in the decision
> JSON, not merely to prose in the reasoning. If the call is "accept the solver", write
> `"accept_hair": [player_ids]` in the decision file; if it is to override, write
> `start`/`bench`. A hair with a prose verdict and no field is treated as UNRESOLVED.**

I did **not** re-run GW27 with the corrected lineup. Re-deciding a gameweek after seeing
that Konaté scored 9 is precisely the leak this whole harness exists to prevent, and the
result stands as scored.
