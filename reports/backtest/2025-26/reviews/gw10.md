## 4. Findings carried to the live pipeline (post-backtest [OPEN]s)

1. **[OPEN] Level calibration — the model under-predicts totals by ~15-25%
   in strong weeks.** Root causes to test: captain-ceiling and bonus-point
   modelling are the weakest links (the bonus proxy has had "no new signal
   since GW1"), and horizon decay may clip high-scorers. Proposed fix for the
   live season: a light environment/level-calibration term (scale the raw xPts
   toward the realised league level) AND/OR a better bonus model. Does not
   change picks; matters for honest EV reporting and hit-gate thresholds.
2. **[OPEN] Bench-order model ignores fixture softness.** GW10 started Senesi
   (MCI away) over Saliba (BUR away) on near-equal flat projections, costing
   ~3. The XI/bench ordering should weight fixture ease (FDR/team-model CS
   probability) more, or expose a manager bench-order override.
3. **[PROCESS] Overlay accuracy — suspensions need the OFFENCE + a confirmed,
   dated ban, not an aggregator headline.** The Ballard "3-match ban" was
   misapplied (he played both weeks). Harmless here, but the discipline
   (knowledge.md already says "ban lengths need the offence") must extend to
   verifying the ban was actually UPHELD/served against the team sheet, and
   defaulting to AVAILABLE when the primary source is ambiguous — an
   erroneous 0.0 could have benched a real starter in a costlier spot.
