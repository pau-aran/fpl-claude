## 5. Improvement proposal (the one change): scope availability overlays to an explicit GW duration

- **Files:** `src/fpl_claude/models/projections.py` (`build_projections`,
  per-GW loop at the `for i, gw in enumerate(gameweeks)` block),
  `src/fpl_claude/models/minutes.py` (overlay dict gains an optional
  `duration_gws` field, default 1), `backtest/overlays.py` pass-through.
- **Why this one, with 4 GWs of evidence.** Two standing candidates:
  - *Overlay-horizon* — three scalps in four weeks, all in the same
    direction (phantom sell-EV on temporarily-doubtful holds: Gvardiol,
    Palmer, Saliba), and it now defeats a correctly-implemented hit gate
    from the input side. The GW2 audit measured the inflation at ~4x
    (+15.45 quoted vs ~+4 real). Every future knock to a premium hold will
    re-trigger it; the manager veto is currently the only working control,
    and a policy that requires a human rescue every week is not a policy.
  - *Tiny-sample p_start* (Ballard) — three scalps too, but decaying: the
    estimate self-corrects as team_games grows (4.10 → 2.42), realized
    marginal cost was 0 this week, and it has not driven a transfer since
    GW2. Still open, no longer the top defect.
  The overlay-horizon fix is better evidenced AND — contrary to the GW3
  note that it "needs per-GW minutes structure" — a bounded version exists
  that does not redesign the minutes model.
- **Fix (~25 lines, no model redesign):** overlays become
  `{"start_share": x, "duration_gws": n, "reason": ...}` with
  `duration_gws` defaulting to 1 (a presser doubt is a one-GW fact unless
  the news says otherwise; a confirmed long-term injury sets it
  explicitly). In `build_projections`, call `minutes_model.estimate_all`
  twice — once with overlays, once without — and in the per-GW loop use the
  overlaid estimate for `i < duration_gws(pid)` and the clean estimate
  after. `xpts_gw{first}` keeps today's behavior; the horizon tail reverts
  to the model's own minutes. Audit trail unchanged (overlay_reason still
  flows).
- **Testable, no future data:** unit test with a synthetic nailed player
  (clean p_start≈1) given `start_share=0.5, duration_gws=1` over an 8-GW
  horizon — expect `xpts_gw` first ≈ half, remaining GWs ≈ clean, and the
  horizon delta vs no-overlay ≈ one GW's worth, not 8x. Second test:
  `duration_gws=8` reproduces today's full-horizon zeroing for genuine
  long-term absences. Deterministic, deadline-time inputs only.
- **Expected effect on the standing cases:** GW4's Saliba sale margin
  collapses from net +5.76 to roughly net (one GW of half-Saliba vs
  Calafiori) − 4 ≈ negative — the gate rejects it with no human rescue.
  GW3's Palmer sale and GW2's +15.45 quote die the same way.
- **Applies from GW5 onward; GW1–4 are not rerun.**
