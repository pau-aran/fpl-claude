## 4. The redirect pattern — second occurrence, judged on evidence

GW4: approval text argued Senesi, constrained solve bought Calafiori.
GW6: reasoning argued the Ekitiké funding route, constrained solve took
the Salah route. Is the post-solve sign-off note sufficient, or does this
need a mechanical fix (e.g. a lock-the-reasoned-route solver mode)?

**Verdict: the process control is sufficient; a mechanical fix is
rejected on the evidence.** Two occurrences, two catches — the re-read
rule fired both times and produced a written, reviewable acceptance both
times. And GW6 is direct evidence AGAINST lock-the-reasoned-route: the
reasoned route was infeasible, so a solver forced to honor it would have
returned no solution (deadline risk) or repaired it with a hit the plan
forbids. The defect GW6 exposes is upstream of the solver: the written
reasoning asserted a funding route without doing the arithmetic. The
proportionate fix is a process line, not a mechanism — **a reasoned
funding route must show its arithmetic (sell price + bank ≥ buy price;
resulting squad quota-legal) before the solve runs.** If a checked-route
still gets redirected, that is the solver disagreeing on merit and the
sign-off note handles it, as designed. Added to knowledge.md; re-judge if
a redirect ever slips through UN-caught.
