## 2. The decision — graded (model + overlay + plan + consensus + duel lens)

A forced triple-out week (Palmer 6wk, Gudmundsson 4wk, Brooks 2-3wk) with
2 FT. Executed Palmer→B.Fernandes + Gudmundsson→Senesi, no hit.

- **Fade-Saka (the headline call) — VINDICATED on process AND outcome.**
  Consensus's most-bought MID was Saka (Arsenal green run); our model rated
  B.Fernandes horizon 23.95 vs Saka 17.0 and we took Fernandes — the
  contrarian value play (crowd was *selling* Fernandes ~240k). Outcome:
  **B.Fernandes 8 vs Saka 7**, and £0.9 cheaper. The model's minutes/underlying
  read beat the reputation pick on EV and on the week. Moneyball rule 1
  working exactly as intended — this is the kind of edge that separates from
  the template.
- **Saliba→Timber veto — 5th refusal, correct again.** Counterfactual:
  Saliba 6 = Timber 6, a dead wash — the −4 the solver wanted would have
  bought zero. Saving the hit was strictly right; the fit-DEF churn never had
  a plan purpose.
- **Ekitiké hold — vindicated by the Isak-out flip.** The planned Ekitiké→Isak
  swap died when Isak got injured; holding Ekitiké as the nailed No.9 spent
  no transfer. He only played 28' this week (1 pt) but the *decision* (don't
  chase Isak's form slump, hold the incumbent) was right and free.
- **Senesi 2 vs unaffordable Gabriel 12 — the week's only "miss", and it's
  variance + a funding wall.** Gabriel (£6.3) hauled a CB 12; Senesi (£5.0)
  blanked at 2. But Gabriel was £0.8 outside budget alongside Fernandes
  (Gabriel+Fernandes £15.3 > £14.5 pot); Senesi was the affordable, sound
  value DEF (consensus #1 defensive target, 28.9% owned) on a good run. A
  defensive haul we couldn't fund is not a process error. NOTE for the run:
  Senesi's BOU fixtures turn hard (MCI(A)5, AVL(A)4) around GW10-11 — revisit.
- **Captain Haaland (8/8)** and the pass on the Salah-v-MUN differential punt
  (Salah returned 2) — both correct.
