## Manager overlay (the human call, outside the algorithm)

**ROLL to the 5-FT cap. The optimizer wanted to demolish the Arsenal bloc for a blank that costs us one slot.**

The unconstrained solve proposed a **four-move teardown** — Gabriel → Guéhi, Timber → Virgil, Rice → Semenyo, Casemiro → Wirtz — worth +7.53 on the horizon, free on our 4 FT. It is refused, and the arithmetic behind the refusal is the whole memo.

**What the model is actually reacting to: BGW31.** Arsenal, Man City, Palace and Wolves blank in GW31 (confirmed since GW24), so Gabriel, Timber, Rice and Haaland all score zero that week, and that zero sits inside the horizon the solver optimises. It is a real fact and the model is right to price it. But the model prices it as *four dead assets* when the actual cost is far smaller, because it has no concept of "I only need eleven players".

**I counted.** Of our 15, **eleven play in GW31**: Roefs and Dúbravka (GK), Senesi, Tarkowski, Konaté (DEF), Enzo, B.Fernandes, Casemiro, Garner (MID), Thiago, Calvert-Lewin (FWD). Only one keeper can start, so the best legal XI we can field in BGW31 today is **1-3-4-2 = ten starters**. The blank costs us **exactly one empty slot**, worth roughly four points — and **one free transfer** converting any blanker into a player fixes it completely.

So the choice is between paying **four transfers now** to pre-solve a one-slot problem five gameweeks away, or paying **one transfer later**. That is not close. And the four-move version is strictly worse than it looks: it sells Rice one week after he returned **14** on the double, and it buys Guéhi and Semenyo — two **Man City** assets who blank in GW31 themselves, so the reshape does not even solve the problem it was motivated by.

**The Free Hit is also off.** The chip advisor still recommends FH at GW31, and I am overriding it for the same reason: a Free Hit that rescues a single slot is a chip spent on four points. **We keep the Free Hit and the Wildcard.** The advisor's WC "4+ change need" signal is likewise an artifact of the same blank — the uncapped optimizer reshapes because GW31 is in its horizon, not because the squad is structurally broken.

**Standing plan set here, to be executed rather than re-litigated weekly: hold the Arsenal bloc through its run (TOT away, CHE home, BHA away, EVE home), and spend ONE free transfer at GW29 or GW30 to convert a single blanker into a GW31 player.** Everything else banks.

**Why not the single-move version either.** Constrained to one move with the bloc locked, the solver's best idea was **Casemiro → Szoboszlai at +1.28** — which is (a) below any bar worth an FT when a known event five weeks out needs the currency, and (b) **Szoboszlai again**, the player we sold at GW24 and have already refused to re-buy twice. The rule stands.

**Rolling 4 → 5 FT hits the cap**, which is the one cost of this decision and is noted deliberately: from GW28 the accumulation is wasted, so GW28 onward must actually spend. That is consistent with the plan above.

**Captain: Haaland, shield restored (26/27).** He tops our board at **6.39** with Enzo second at 5.84 — a 0.55 margin, which clears the ≥0.5 threshold the GW25 review set, and in the direction of the incumbent so no deviation question arises. His February knock resolved pre-deadline (omitted from the FA Cup squad 14 Feb, then Guardiola on 20 Feb indicating he is fit), City are at **home to Newcastle** and have no European midweek. Worth recording that this is the first week the community's captain is **not** ours — FFScout's Scout Squad went Palmer with João Pedro as vice — and we are holding on our own numbers, which is the same discipline that produced 24/24 before GW25 broke it.

**Bench hairs — three margins, all pointing at the same player, and I am accepting the solver on all three.** Calvert-Lewin is the man being displaced in every case: Casemiro 0.12 ahead, Garner 0.16, Konaté 0.22. So the real question is not three duels but one — *should Calvert-Lewin start?* — and the answer is no: he is coming off an illness week where he recorded **zero minutes**, Leeds are away at Chelsea, and any of the three alternatives is a nailed 90-minute player. Starting the eleven the solver picked. Recording it explicitly because the GW25 lesson was that an unnamed hair is the dangerous one, not a disagreed-with one.
