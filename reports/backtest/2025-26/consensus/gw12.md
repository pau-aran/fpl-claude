## Headline: the international break broke three template mids/defenders

GW12 was defined by injuries picked up on international duty, not by fixtures. Three
of the most-owned assets in the game went down or into doubt in the same window:

- **Gabriel (Arsenal DEF)** — thigh tear for Brazil (limped off v Senegal, 15 Nov),
  **out ~4 weeks**, missing the north London derby and beyond. A league-league-best
  defence's linchpin, ~30%+ owned.
- **Antoine Semenyo (Bournemouth MID)** — aggravated an ankle ligament for Ghana,
  **absent from the squad**, no clear return date (and Ghana at AFCON from 21 Dec).
- **Enzo Fernández (Chelsea MID)** — a *pressure point* among elite managers
  (fixture-driven doubt, not injury); ended up a top-5 most-SOLD mid.

FFScout framed Gabriel, Enzo and Semenyo as "the common pressure points among elite
FPL managers heading into GW12." The transfer market was a scramble to resolve them.
