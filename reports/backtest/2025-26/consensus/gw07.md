## Headline: Haaland again — but the last exit before the October break

GW7 was the final round before the two-week October international break, which
made it a double decision point: captain Haaland at **Brentford (A), Sunday
5 Oct**, AND settle the wildcard/roll question before two weeks of price moves
and injury risk. Haaland entered on 38 pts in his last three (5 goals, 16-pt
GW6 haul vs Burnley, ~4M captains). Friday's opener BOU–FUL gave Semenyo
holders an early captain alternative.

## 2. Transfer market of the week (pre-deadline counts)

Per Fantasy Football Fix "FPL Gameweek 7 Transfers – Top Moves & Transfer Tips"
(pre-deadline, "ahead of Gameweek 7") and premierleague.com's Scout GW7
transfer radar:

**In:**
- **Haaland £14.4m — most-bought again, ~500,000 in** on top of GW6's 865k;
  "looking back to his best", 38 pts / 5 goals in three. The market finished
  converging on him as a must-have.
- **Senesi ~300,000 in** — top-scoring defensive asset in the game; defender
  buys dominated by **Senesi, Guéhi and Gabriel** (Arsenal's 6-GW fixture run
  turning green from GW7 was the Scout radar's lead item, Gabriel its top pick
  vs set-piece-leaky West Ham).
- Midfield buys: **Saka, Semenyo, Sarr, Anthony (Burnley, 5th-top FPL scorer
  at that point), Eze** — Saka flagged by Fix as the go-to Salah replacement at
  just 6.3% ownership.
- Forwards: **Gyökeres** alongside Haaland among top forward buys. Woltemade
  (£7.2m, ~18.6% ownership, price rising pre-GW7) was climbing but NOT yet a
  top-mover — his mass bandwagon (and the Woltemade-vs-Gordon debate) is GW8+
  content (FLAGGED: FFScout ran it 2025-10-09, post-deadline).

**Out:**
- **Salah — most-sold, ~397,000 out**, after 321k out in GW6: a genuine
  two-week exodus (16 pts in his prior three by Fix's count). Fix noted **nine
  of the top ten midfield transfer combinations involved selling a Liverpool
  asset** — Gakpo heavily sold too, after Liverpool's back-to-back defeats
  (Palace 2025-09-27, Galatasaray 2025-09-30).
- Forward sells: **Wood, Ekitiké, Watkins, Mateta** — Ekitiké still being
  dumped even though his domestic ban ended (see §4).
- Palmer sells rolled on: ruled out until after the October break (below), so
  remaining holders were selling into the deadline.

## 3. Strategy roadmaps in circulation that week

- **The GW7 wildcard window:** FFScout ran "FPL Gameweek 7 Wildcard: Pros, cons
  + best drafts" (2025-09-30, URL-dated); premierleague.com published an "FPL
  expert's Gameweek 7 Wildcard squad"; allaboutfpl a GW7 wildcard guide
  (2025/09, URL-dated); Full90FPL GW7 wildcard templates/cheat sheet. The pro
  case: Arsenal's fixture swing from GW7, Liverpool disruption, dead Palmer
  money. The con case, explicitly argued: **wait until after the international
  break** — two weeks of price changes and injury news could wreck a
  pre-break wildcard, and some GW7 fixtures (e.g. holds with one good fixture
  left) argued for patience.
- **Roll into the break:** the non-wildcard advice mirrored it — bank the free
  transfer through the break where possible; GW7 was framed as the week to
  avoid unforced hits before two weeks of new information.
- **Palmer:** Chelsea confirmed pre-deadline (Maresca pressers, ~2025-09-30 to
  10-02) that Palmer would be **rested for ~three weeks until after the
  October break**, missing BHA, Benfica and LIV, with Forest (2025-10-18)
  the targeted return. (FLAGGED post-deadline: the "further six weeks / late
  November" escalation came mid-October and was NOT knowable at this deadline.)
- **Template reveals:** Zophar's GW7 reveal + transfer plan (FFScout,
  2025-10-02); **FPL General's GW7 reveal (FFScout, 2025-10-03)** — General
  running his season-long **no-Salah, no-Haaland structure** (three top-500
  finishes), the highest-profile dissent from the Haaland template.

## 4. Effective-ownership context for OUR holds

- **Haaland (we hold, captain candidate):** post-GW6/GW7 rush he is effectively
  template with a ~60% captain-poll share. Captaining him at BRE (A) is the
  zero-EO-risk move; NOT captaining him is the single biggest rank gamble
  available this week. Market fully agrees with our hold.
- **Palmer (we hold, market selling):** confirmed out until GW8 at the
  earliest. The crowd is out or leaving; holding costs a bench slot for one GW
  before the break but carries zero EO downside while he's unavailable. The
  pre-deadline info supported "hold if no free transfer need, reassess over
  the break" — sell pressure was price-driven, not points-driven.
- **Ekitiké (we hold, back from ban at CHE (A)):** the market kept selling him
  (top-4 forward sells) into his return — ban over, but Isak was pushing for a
  first Liverpool PL start in exactly this fixture, and Liverpool's form
  wobble cut appeal. Low and falling EO: any Ekitiké return at Stamford
  Bridge would be a differential gain; a benching costs little vs the field.
- **No Salah (our stance):** vindicated by the market — most-sold player two
  weeks running (~397k this week). Zero EO pressure to buy back before the
  break; the crowd's replacement money went to Saka/Semenyo, not back to him.
