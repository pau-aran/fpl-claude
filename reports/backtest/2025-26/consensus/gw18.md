The dominant pre-deadline story was **Bruno Fernandes' hamstring** (pulled at Villa, GW17;

triple-owners), a value Bournemouth/Chelsea mid (Semenyo, Neto), and simply banking for the
returnee window. Captaincy stayed Haaland on a soft-ish Forest away.
