# Community consensus — 2025/26 GW6 (deadline Sat 2025-09-27 ~10:00 UTC)

*Point-in-time reconstruction for the backtest. Every claim below is sourced to
content datable BEFORE the deadline (article URLs carry publication dates);
anything only verifiable post-deadline is flagged as such. Purpose: measure the
effective-ownership landscape our GW6 decision would have faced.*

## Headline: an all-in Haaland week

GW6's defining fixture was **Man City vs Burnley (H)** — Burnley having conceded
the most shots, shots in box, shots on target and xGC in the league to that
point. Haaland entered on 6 goals in 5 GWs (46 pts, top overall scorer).

## 2. Transfer market of the week (pre-deadline counts)

Per premierleague.com's Scout GW6 transfer radar (pub. 2025-09-26) and Fantasy
Football Fix "Biggest FPL Transfer Moves Before Gameweek 6":

**In:**
- **Haaland £14.3m — most-bought, 865,000+ transfers in** ("get Haaland before
  BUR(H)" was the week's explicit roadmap; GW6 called "one of the busiest
  transfer weeks of the season").
- **Semenyo — 3rd most-bought, 531,238 in** (20 pts in prior 3, goal + assist,
  strong underlying incl. 32 DefCon) — the value-riser bandwagon of the week.
- Woltemade (£7.0m NEW) was flagged as a cheap early punt at ~2% ownership
  (101greatgoals GW5 must-buys, mid-Sept 2025) but was NOT yet a mass move —
  his 650-900k bandwagon came in GW8-9 (post-dating this deadline; noted for
  later backtest weeks).

**Out (funding the Haaland rush):**
- **Ekitiké — 500,000+ out** (suspended for GW6 after his red card v Southampton,
  Carabao Cup, 2025-09-23).
- **Salah — ~445,000 out** — a genuine early exodus after his slow start (30 pts
  through GW5, only 4th among midfielders; premierleague.com ran "What to do
  with Salah in Fantasy?" that week).
- **Watkins — ~341,000 out.**
- Palmer sells accelerating: subbed off 21' at Man Utd (2025-09-20) aggravating
  his groin injury; Maresca's "out six more weeks" line and GW6 preview content
  ("...and a Palmer replacement", Gulf News GW6 tips) were live before the
  deadline.

## 3. Strategy roadmaps in circulation that week

- **Wildcard timing:** FFScout ran "Is Gameweek 6 the best time to Wildcard?"
  (2025-09-18, URL-dated); allaboutfpl published GW6 wildcard drafts and
  position-by-position target lists (Sept 2025). GW6 was a widely discussed
  early-wildcard window — the trigger being funds needed for Haaland + the
  Ekitiké/Palmer/Salah disruption.
- **Defensive stacking:** allaboutfpl's GW6 wildcard targets prioritised
  **Gabriel and Arsenal/Man City defensive cover**; Leeds (2 CS in 5, decent
  run) flagged as budget cover.
- **The Haaland roadmap:** the dominant multi-week plan was simply "own and
  captain Haaland through the City run starting BUR(H)" — reflected in both the
  865k rush and the TC activations.
- FPL General's GW6 team reveal + transfer plans published 2025-09-26
  (FFScout, URL-dated) — the template-proxy reveal of the week.
