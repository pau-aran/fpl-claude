## Headline: Haaland runaway again — and Palmer's return bet dies on the Friday presser

GW8 was the first round back from the two-week October international break, so
the market arrived with two weeks of price moves, injury flags and — critically
— a wave of **wildcard activations** already banked or being pressed. Haaland
came back on fire (fifth straight PL goal at Brentford in GW7, then a Norway
hat-trick over the break) and was **home to Everton (MCI v EVE, H)** — the
easiest captain call of the season so far. The defining news event landed
**Friday 17 October** — Maresca's "I was wrong" presser ruling **Palmer out
for a further six weeks** — which detonated the whole hold-Palmer-for-a-GW8-
return thesis the day before our deadline.

## 2. Transfer market of the week (pre-deadline counts)

Per Fantasy Football Fix "Top FPL Managers' Transfer Trends for Gameweek 8",
allaboutfpl's GW8 pieces and premierleague.com's Scout GW8 transfer radar
(all pre-deadline, "ahead of Gameweek 8"):

**In — a post-break, wildcard-fuelled reshuffle:**
- **Saka — most-bought, ~316,000+ in.** Fix/premierleague framed Arsenal's run
  (FUL A, CRY H, BUR A, SUN A) as the green swing to buy into; **Fernandes → Saka**
  was the single top transfer combination (~2.5% of all moves). Still sub-10%
  owned, so a high-upside near-template add.
- **Woltemade — the bandwagon goes live, ~545k–600k in** (Fix ~545k; allaboutfpl
  cited 599,476, second-most-transferred-in overall). The Woltemade-v-Gordon
  debate that opened ~9 Oct resolved decisively toward Woltemade; **Richarlison
  → Woltemade** a top combo. (FLAGGED post-deadline: premierleague's GW8 stats
  quoted ~903,000 total owners — a post-deadline snapshot, not the pre-deadline
  buy count.)
- **Sarr** (Grealish → Sarr a top combo), **Gabriel** (top defensive target on
  the Arsenal run) and **Nick Pope** (most-targeted keeper on wildcards — 5 clean
  sheets in 7, top-scoring GK) rounded out the template buys.

**Out:**
- **Salah — again among the most-sold / top transfer out**, the post-break
  exodus rolling on despite his Man Utd home fixture (the fixture gave holders a
  captaincy case, but wildcarders kept ditching him amid Liverpool's form
  wobble).
- **Fernandes ~240,000+ out** and **Richarlison ~274,000+ out** (a minutes risk
  managers were happy to move on from) were the other headline sells.
- **Palmer — a fresh sell wave**, triggered by the Friday six-week news (§3/§4):
  managers who had held for a GW8 return now had a concrete reason to cash out.

## 3. Strategy roadmaps in circulation that week

- **GW8 = the big wildcard week.** With the break's injury flags and fixture
  swings, this was the season's first mass-wildcard round. FFScout ran
  "FPL Gameweek 8 Wildcard: pros/cons + best drafts" (2025-10-06), "How to
  future-proof your GW8 Wildcard" (2025-10-15) and Tom Freeman's GW8 wildcard
  team (2025-10-17); premierleague published an "FPL expert's GW8 Wildcard squad"
  (panellist Pranil Sheth activating his); Fix, allaboutfpl and 101greatgoals all
  ran GW8 wildcard blueprints. The rationale: injury insurance post-break plus
  buying the green fixture swings now.
- **Fixture-run targets turning green (GW8-12):** **Arsenal** (Saka/Gabriel —
  FUL, CRY, BUR, SUN), **Newcastle** (Pope + Woltemade), **West Ham** (Bowen —
  three of next four vs sides 15th-or-lower, per the Scout radar) and
  **Bournemouth** (Semenyo captaincy backing) were the runs the market bought.
- **Template composition after the break:** forwards coalescing on
  **Haaland + Woltemade + João Pedro**; midfield on **Saka, Semenyo, Sarr**;
  Pope/Raya and the Arsenal/Sunderland defensive blocks the value picks.
- **Palmer — the roadmap-defining injury:** Maresca's Friday 17 Oct presser
  ("I was wrong") pushed Palmer's return to **~end of November** (six more weeks),
  killing the GW7-era plan of holding through the break for a GW8 return v Forest.
  This is the mirror image of the GW7 flag — what was "post-deadline, not knowable"
  at GW7 became **hard pre-deadline news** at GW8.
- **Template reveals:** **FPL General's GW8 reveal (FFScout, 2025-10-18)** —
  still committed to his **no-Salah, no-Haaland** structure; he brought in
  **Saka (C), Senesi and Pope**, selling Reijnders, Cucurella and E. Martínez.
  (FLAGGED: the "72 pts / +1M rank" outcome in that piece is post-deadline.)

## 4. Effective-ownership context for OUR holds

- **Haaland (we hold, captain):** post-break he is maximally template with a
  **71% captain-poll share** and MCI v EVE (H). Captaining him is the zero-EO-risk
  move; NOT captaining him is the single biggest rank gamble available. The only
  contrarian is Salah-on-Man-Utd, a pure punt. Market fully agrees — captain
  Haaland.
- **Palmer (we hold — the bet resolves AGAINST us):** the hold-vs-sell wager was
  "does he return GW8?" Friday's presser answered **no — out ~six more weeks to
  late November.** He now carries **near-zero EO** (the crowd is out and a fresh
  sell wave is on), so holding costs us nothing versus the field *this week* — but
  it is dead money and a permanent bench slot for a month-plus. With **2 FT in
  hand and no injury excuse left**, GW8 is the clean week to move him; the market
  read is unambiguous sell/downgrade.
- **Ekitiké → Isak swap (we weigh):** the market is **not** rushing to Isak —
  he has one goal in nine and blanked twice for Sweden over the break, and
  Liverpool are wobbling into LIV v MUN (H). Ekitiké remained the more-backed
  Liverpool attacker of the two. Both are low-EO; swapping into Isak's form slump
  now looks premature versus consensus — the crowd would hold/keep Ekitiké or go
  Wirtz, not chase Isak. Low downside either way, but no EO pressure to make the
  swap.
- **No Salah (our stance):** broadly still vindicated — Salah remained a top
  transfer *out* as wildcarders ditched him. BUT this is the one week with a
  fixture-based EO wrinkle: **LIV v MUN (H)** is his favourite opponent and he was
  2nd on the captain metrics, so a Salah haul this specific GW would sting
  non-owners more than usual. A one-week fixture risk to log, not a reason to buy
  back into a player the template is actively selling.
