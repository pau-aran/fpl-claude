weekend (Sun 22 Mar) — and we own the **Arsenal triple plus Haaland**, i.e. **four blanking assets**,

## 4. Chip watch

**DGW26 is behind us; BGW31 is now the only live chip question, and GW28 is where positioning starts.**

- **BGW31 — the confirmed shape.** Gameweek 31 (weekend of **Sat 21 / Sun 22 Mar**) clashes with the
  **EFL Cup final, Arsenal v Man City at Wembley on Sun 22 Mar**. **Arsenal, Man City, Crystal Palace
  and Wolves blank**; the other 16 clubs play. The eight GW31 fixtures are:
  **Bournemouth v Man Utd (Fri)**, **Brighton v Liverpool**, **Fulham v Burnley**, **Everton v
  Chelsea**, **Leeds v Brentford**, **Newcastle v Sunderland**, **Aston Villa v West Ham**,
  **Spurs v Nottingham Forest**.
- **The pre-deadline nuance most managers miss.** FFScout asked on **25 Feb** — two days before our
  deadline — "**Is there a chance Man City + Palace could still play in Blank Gameweek 31?**" The
  answer is not a flat no: the **Man City v Crystal Palace** fixture is the one still to be
  rescheduled, and the window floated for it was the **midweek of 7/8 April**, contingent on both
  clubs' European exits before the quarter-finals. Two consequences for us: (a) the four-team blank
  is the correct planning assumption but is **not 100% locked for City/Palace** at this deadline, and
  (b) whenever MCI v CRY is replayed it becomes a **later City and Palace double** — a reason to hold
  the chip inventory loosely rather than commit a Free Hit to GW31 today.
- **What the community is actually doing (sourced pre-deadline).** The reference piece is FFScout's
  **5 Feb** article by three-time top-200 finisher **Lateriser**, "**Players to buy if you're
  'dead-ending' to FPL Blank Gameweek 31**". Its read: **Liverpool, Fulham and Bournemouth "will
  definitely be the teams to target"**, with **Newcastle and Brentford** also worth checking; **Igor
  Thiago, Lewis Hall and Kevin Schade** are "expected to become popular"; cheap defender **James
  Hill** and **Morgan Rogers** are named buys; and **Semenyo, Sarr and Daniel Muñoz** are explicitly
  framed as **short-term "dead-end" buys** — good now, zero in GW31 — only "should a manager either
  have lots of transfers, enough Blank Gameweek 31 players, or intend to use a chip at that moment".
  So the community's GW28 frame is a **fork**: either *dead-end* (buy blanking assets now, accept the
  GW31 hole, fix it with a chip) or *build early* (start converting blankers into the eight playing
  clubs from GW28-30 and take GW31 on free transfers).
- **Free Hit vs Wildcard — the honest state of the evidence at this deadline.** I could **not** source
  a settled pre-deadline consensus. What is defensible on 27 Feb: our own inventory (second-half
  WC / FH / BB / TC, per the two-chip-sets rule that unlocked at GW20) covers BGW31 either way; a
  **Free Hit** is the low-commitment answer (one week, squad returns intact) and a **Wildcard around
  GW29-30** is the high-commitment answer that also fixes the Bruno G. hole and re-shapes for the
  run-in. The counter-argument that dominates later coverage — *save the Free Hit because GW34 (FA
  Cup semi-final weekend) will be a bigger blank, wildcard into a DGW33 instead* — comes from
  **March-dated** articles and is **excluded as post-deadline** (see hygiene notes). The only
  forward-looking structure knowable on 27 Feb is calendrical: the **FA Cup fifth round is Sat 7
  March** and the **quarter-finals Sat 4 April**, so further blanks/doubles in the GW33-34 region are
  *anticipatable* but not yet allocatable to named clubs.
- **Our practical position, stated plainly.** We hold **four blanking assets** (Saliba, Timber, Rice,
  Haaland) — the maximum. Our cheap half (Roefs, Dúbravka, Senesi, Tarkowski, Garner, Thiago,
  B.Fernandes, Konaté, Enzo, Calvert-Lewin) **all play in GW31**. That means BGW31 is a **four-slot**
  problem, not a fifteen-slot one — which is precisely the size of problem a **Free Hit** solves
  cleanly and a Wildcard over-solves. **No chip action at GW28**; the decision point is GW29-30.
  **Bench Boost** is not live (knowledge.md: BB only on a DGW).
- **What GW28 should NOT do:** buy any Arsenal, Man City, Palace or Wolves asset (Gabriel, Gyökeres,
  Eze, O'Reilly, Semenyo, Guéhi, Aït-Nouri, Muñoz, Sarr all fail this test), and do not sell a
  blank-proof asset (Thiago, B.Fernandes, Konaté, Tarkowski, Senesi) to fund one that blanks.
