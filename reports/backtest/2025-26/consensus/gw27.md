# Community consensus — 2025/26 GW27 (deadline Sat 2026-02-21 13:30 UTC)

*Point-in-time reconstruction. Every claim is sourced to content datable BEFORE the deadline.
GW27 is a Saturday-to-Monday round: first KO is the **15:00 GMT** cluster (Villa v Leeds, Brentford v
Brighton, Chelsea v Burnley) ⇒ deadline **13:30 UTC**; West Ham v Bournemouth 17:30 and Man City v
Newcastle 20:00 close the Saturday; Sunday brings Palace v Wolves and Forest v Liverpool (14:00) and
the **north London derby at 16:30**; Everton v Man Utd closes the round Mon 23 Feb 20:00. This round
lands at the far end of the season's worst congestion block — **GW25 (7-8 Feb), a midweek GW26 (10-12
Feb), an FA Cup round (14-15 Feb) and a European play-off midweek (17-19 Feb) inside fifteen days** —
and it opens the four-round run-in to **BGW31**.*

## Headline: Bruno G. is gone until April (the forced sale we have deferred twice), Haaland's knock clears in time but the crowd has moved its armband to Palmer, and Arsenal walk into the derby on a fourth game in eleven days after a DGW26 that returned two draws

The congestion converted into exactly what the plan feared, and it took **our** player. **Bruno
Guimarães tore a hamstring in the closing minutes of Newcastle's 2-1 win at Spurs on Tue 10 Feb** and
Howe has ruled him out **until after the March international break** ("Around the next international
break — the March international break — that he will be back"); premierleague.com's piece is headlined
"**Guimarães out until April**", with reporting spanning six-to-seven weeks (Newcastle's hope) to ten
(the pessimistic read). The GW23/GW24 "bench him and decide next week" holding pattern is over: this is
a **multi-week hole and a forced sale**, and it is the natural funding line for a **BGW31-playing**
midfielder. Second: **Haaland's fitness scare resolved pre-deadline** — withdrawn at half-time of the
3-0 win over Fulham (Wed 11 Feb) with a knock, then **left out of the FA Cup squad on Sat 14 Feb**
(Guardiola: "Erling's not 100%… it's not a big issue"), but at the **Fri 20 Feb presser Guardiola
nodded when asked if he was fully fit**, and City — top-eight in the UCL league phase — have **no
European game on either side of this fixture**. The armband market has nonetheless moved: **FFScout's
Scout Squad captained Cole Palmer**, with **João Pedro** vice, on the back of Chelsea hosting Burnley,
and **Semenyo has outscored Haaland 33-24 over the last five gameweeks**. Third: **Arsenal's DGW26
returned 1-1 at Brentford and 2-2 at Wolves** — two blown leads, no clean sheets — and they now play
their **fourth game in eleven days** away at a **Spurs side in freefall**: 16th, winless in five,
**Thomas Frank sacked and Igor Tudor installed as interim on 17 Feb**, and **captain Romero suspended**
for the derby. Arteta reports **no new injury concerns**; Saliba, Timber and Rice are all in the
predicted XI.

  The Moneyball question is not *whether* to sell but *into what*: the answer should be a midfielder at a

  Muñoz — Palace, and any Arsenal add). Overlay 0.02 / duration 6.

  Written for the review: the challenger this week is a **managed-minutes** premium we do not own and

## 4. Chip watch

- **DGW26 is closed.** Arsenal's double delivered **1-1 at Brentford (12 Feb)** and **2-2 at Wolves (18
  Feb)** — two blown leads, no clean sheets. Whatever was done with the queued TC, the read forward is
  the same: **do not carry a "the Arsenal fixture load will pay" thesis into the run-in**; it has been
  tested and it produced draws.
- **BGW31 is the live chip question from this week onward.** **Arsenal, Man City, Crystal Palace and
  Wolves blank** because the **EFL Cup final is Arsenal v Man City** (finalists known since the semis in
  early Feb), which kills **ARS v WOL** and **MCI v CRY** on that weekend. **Of our fifteen, only FOUR
  blank** — Saliba, Timber, Rice and Haaland. **Eleven play** (Roefs, Dúbravka, Tarkowski, Senesi,
  Konaté, Enzo, B.Fernandes, Garner, Thiago, Calvert-Lewin and whatever replaces Bruno G.).
- **That materially weakens the Free Hit case.** The standing rule is **WC/FH only for a genuine 4+
  change need or a Blank/Double**, and a BGW31 with **eleven starters already** is a *3-4 transfer*
  problem, not a chip problem — solvable with the FTs banked between here and GW31 if we stop buying
  into blanking clubs now. The counter-argument is quality, not quantity: the four who blank include our
  **captain** and two premium defenders, so a no-chip BGW31 fields a much weaker XI than the raw count
  suggests. **No decision this week; the decision is due around GW29-30.**
- **The positioning move that IS available now:** the Bruno G. sale is a free chance to enter a
  **GW31-playing** club. The pre-deadline candidates that satisfy both "good now" and "plays GW31" are
  **João Pedro (£7.7m, CHE)**, **Sánchez/Chalobah (CHE)**, **Anderson (£5.3m, NFO — new-manager reset)**,
  **Kelleher (£4.6m, BRE)**, **Petrović (£4.5m, BOU)** and **Casemiro (£5.6m, MUN)**. Conversely
  **Semenyo (MCI), Muñoz (CRY), Sarr (CRY), Gabriel/Raya/Saka/Gyökeres (ARS)** are all blank-side buys.
- **Honest limitation:** dedicated BGW31 Free-Hit drafts and "who to keep, who to sell" content is
  **mid-March** material and therefore **post-deadline** — at GW27 the community chatter is still
  fixture-ticker-level. Nothing in this section is sourced to a March article; it is built from the
  pre-deadline blank confirmation plus our own squad arithmetic.
