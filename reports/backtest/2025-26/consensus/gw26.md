# Community consensus — 2025/26 GW26, the DOUBLE (deadline Tue 2026-02-10 18:00 UTC)

*Point-in-time reconstruction. Every claim is sourced to content datable BEFORE the deadline.
GW26 is the season's FIRST double gameweek and it is **stretched over nine days**: first KO Tue 10
Feb 19:30 (Chelsea v Leeds, Everton v Bournemouth, Spurs v Newcastle), the bulk on Wed 11 Feb,
**Brentford v Arsenal Thu 12 Feb 20:00**, and the round closes with **Wolves v Arsenal Wed 18 Feb
20:00** — the double leg. **Arsenal and Wolves are the only doublers** (their GW31 fixture was moved
forward because both GW31 and the EFL Cup final weekend collide). The whole round is played, and
every chip decision made, off team news available before **18:00 on Tuesday**.*

> **The single most important hygiene fact of this week:** because the deadline is Tue 18:00 and
> Arsenal do not play until **Thu 12 Feb**, **Arteta's pre-Brentford press conference (Wed 11 Feb)
> is POST-deadline**. The last usable Arsenal team news is the **pre-Sunderland presser (Fri 6 Feb)**
> plus the **GW25 round itself (6-8 Feb)**, which is complete and therefore fully usable. We are
> committing a chip with two days *less* team news than a normal week — and with **zero** team news
> for a second leg that is **eight days after the deadline**.

## Headline: the first double is a one-club double — Arsenal — and the community has consolidated on **Triple Captain Gabriel**; our 3-club cap makes it a **Rice-vs-Timber** decision, and the pre-deadline evidence does not agree with our model

Arsenal go to **Brentford (Thu 12 Feb)** and **Wolves (Wed 18 Feb)**: the league leaders, playing the
division's **bottom side** in the second leg, avoiding every other top team, with a defence that has
kept **four clean sheets in five**. The community treats this as the cleanest Triple Captain window
available and has converged hard on **Gabriel (£7.1m)** — **57% of the FFScout GW26 captain poll**
(allaboutfpl's poll has him at 52%), with **Rice 2nd on 17%**, the only other player above 5%. About
**25% of FFScout poll respondents say they are playing Triple Captain** this week; Bench Boost and
Free Hit are explicitly unpopular *because bigger doubles and blanks are known to be coming*.

The rotation question — which is the entire risk of a TC — resolves better than feared on pre-deadline
evidence. **There are six clear days between the two legs**, and the only Arsenal fixture in between is
the **FA Cup 4th round at home to Wigan (Sun 15 Feb)**, a tie FFScout says "**Arteta is expected to
tinker with**" — i.e. the cup absorbs the rotation instead of the league legs. Critically, **there is no
European tie in the window**: Arsenal won the Champions League league phase with a **perfect 8/8**,
finished top, and are seeded **straight into the March Round of 16**, skipping the 17/18 Feb knockout
play-off — which is precisely why the Premier League could schedule Wolves v Arsenal on **Wed 18 Feb**
at all. The residual risk is domestic and datable: **four games in ten days** (Brentford 12th, Wigan
15th, Wolves 18th, **North London derby at Spurs Sat 21 Feb**), which makes **leg 2 the rest window**.

That is where our model and the market part company. Our board ranks **Timber 9.49 > Rice 9.02 > Raya
8.29**. The community ranks **Gabriel ≈ Rice (11.2 projected each) > Timber (9.9)** — and **Timber is
the only Arsenal starter carrying a written rotation warning**: FFScout, 6 Feb, "*Saliba is worth
considering, though there is a **risk of rotation for Timber** with games coming thick and fast*."
Meanwhile **Merino is out for the season** (foot stress fracture, surgery, ~4 months), Ødegaard has a
muscle niggle and Saka a hip — which leaves **Rice and Zubimendi as the only fit senior central
midfielders**, with Nørgaard (101 minutes all season) behind them. **Rice is the least rotatable player
in the squad.** If we triple an owned Arsenal asset, the pre-deadline evidence points at **Rice**, not
at the model's Timber.

Elsewhere the week is quiet and mostly good for us: **Bruno G.'s ankle doubt is resolved** (full 90 in
GW25), **Calafiori's return turns out to be at LEFT-BACK — not a Timber/Saliba threat**, and our only
new doubt is **Calvert-Lewin (illness)**. The Wolves half of the double is, on the community's own
verdict, close to unusable.
