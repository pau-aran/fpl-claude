## Headline: Haaland loses the armband in the community for the first time this season — and it is no longer only about form

Three things define GW25 pre-deadline. **First, the armband moved.** Fantasy Football Scout's GW25
captain poll has **Bruno Fernandes above 50% for the second week running** (five assists in his last
four), **João Pedro ~11.5%**, **Gabriel third — and Haaland only FOURTH**; the Scout Selection names
**Fernandes captain, Gabriel vice**, with Haaland absent from it. The reason is now *two*-sided:
Haaland goes to **Anfield** on **18 points from six appearances** (one goal, one assist, 3.0 ppm
against a 7.4 pts-per-start season average), **and** Guardiola, asked directly on Fri 6 Feb whether
he starts, answered **"I don't know, we'll decide tomorrow, but I always say Erling is the best"** —
after **benching him for the Carabao semi 2nd leg** on Wed 4 Feb while **Marmoush scored twice**. The
benching reads as a *rest for Anfield* (which argues he starts), but a manager who won't commit plus
an in-form deputy plus the GW23 Wolves-benching precedent is a real tail. **Second, Saka is out** —
hip, from the **warm-up at Elland Road on 31 Jan**, missed the cup semi, Arteta on 6 Feb "getting much
better… hopefully very soon" with no date and a "3 weeks" report — and he is **the main sale funding
the whole market's GW25 moves**, with the crowd's replacements being **Rice**, **Enzo** and
**B.Fernandes**: three players *we already own*. **Third, DGW26 is locked in and the pre-deadline
evidence on Arsenal rotation is benign for us**: Arteta started **Saliba, Timber, Gabriel and Rice** in
the cup semi on 3 Feb, his injury load is entirely in attack (Merino out months, Saka, Dowman, Ødegaard
a late call), and Arsenal's natural rest slot in the DGW26 fortnight is the **FA Cup 4th round home tie
v Wigan on 14/15 Feb** — *between* the two legs — with **no European tie** in that window.
