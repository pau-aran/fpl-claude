## Headline: the window closes with Konaté BACK (hold, not sell), the Man Utd entry the template play (Mbeumo the value buy), a NEW hole at Mateta (AC Milan), and Haaland's armband facing its stiffest test yet

The pivotal funding question resolved the right way: **Ibrahima Konaté is BACK.** He cut short his
compassionate leave — his father died the week before GW23 — and called Slot early this week
("I want to be back to help the team against Newcastle") to help Liverpool through a **defensive
injury crisis** (Van Dijk, Gomez, Frimpong and Bradley all out); he trained twice and is in the
predicted XI. Slot (Fri 30 Jan): "Hoping and expecting to have Ibou back. I cannot guarantee this."
So the returnee our board treated as the natural funding sale is instead a **HOLD** — the sale must
come from elsewhere. Meanwhile the template has swung hard onto **Man Utd**: after an unbeaten
six-game run under Carrick — including wins over **City (GW22)** and **Arsenal (GW23, Mbeumo 10 in
a 3-2 win)** — the two most-transferred-in players are **Bruno Fernandes (No. 1, £9.5m, 583k in)**
and **Mbeumo (No. 2, £8.3m, 483k in)**, with the consensus explicitly "invest in Man Utd from GW24"
into their **home Fulham (FDR2)** opener. This is the entry our banked FTs were FOR. But the window
closes with a **new hole**: **Jean-Philippe Mateta is in an AC Milan transfer saga and has been left
out of the Palace squad for Forest (A)** — pre-deadline consensus is he "will not play GW24." And
the armband faces its softest case of the season: **Haaland goes to Spurs (A) in his worst FPL form
of 2025/26** (1 goal, 15 pts in six GWs), with **Bruno Fernandes**, **Gabriel** and a **home Salah v
a depleted Newcastle** all live alternatives — though Haaland is back scoring (UCL, Wed 28 Jan) after
the GW23 Wolves benching, so it is a form question, not an availability one. Template play: enter the
Man Utd wave, resolve Mateta, weigh the armband harder than usual.
