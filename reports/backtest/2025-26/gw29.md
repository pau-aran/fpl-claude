## Manager overlay (the human call, outside the algorithm)

**Roefs → Sánchez (1 of 4 FT, forced). Captain B.Fernandes — the shield comes off, properly this time.**

**The forced move.** Roefs pulled a hamstring making a save at Bournemouth in GW28 and is priced **0.20 with `duration_gws` 2** — a scoped multi-week absence, not a doubt, so the GW25 rule ("bench a doubt, don't sell it") does not apply: there is nothing to keep the option on. A first-choice keeper who will not play GW29 or GW30 is a dead slot in a week we cannot afford one.

**Why Sánchez and not Pickford.** Pickford is the better keeper on our board (4.10 v 3.65, and Everton are home to Burnley with a 9–11 day rest gap and no cup tie). I tried to buy him and **the solve came back infeasible: bank £0.2m plus Roefs' £4.9m sale is £5.1m against Pickford's £5.6m.** He is £0.5m out of reach. Sánchez (£4.9m, Chelsea) is the funded upgrade, is nailed, and — the part that matters for the next three weeks — **plays in BGW31**. Recording the attempt rather than presenting the second choice as the first: the thin bank is a real constraint we created by buying Gabriel at GW25, and this is where it bit.

**Captain: B.Fernandes over Haaland (4.98 v 3.91).** This is the second captaincy deviation of the season and, unlike GW25's, it clears the bar the GW25 review set with room to spare — **both** conditions are met:

1. **Availability flag, and a severe one.** Haaland is priced **0.50**. He **missed GW28 entirely** with an undisclosed knock, **missed training on Monday 2 March**, and Guardiola's Tuesday presser said the call would be made *after* our deadline: "not a big issue… Erling feels much better, but yesterday we didn't train and now we have training and will decide today." His expected minutes are **41.7**.
2. **Board margin 1.07**, well past the ≥0.5 threshold — and it is a *minutes* gap, not a form opinion.

The community has already moved: the Scout dropped him from the GW29 Scout Selection and captained Semenyo. GW25 taught me the difference between a switch justified by a flag and a switch executed on a 0.30 margin; this one is the former with the margin attached.

**Haaland is BENCHED-BUT-KEPT — held in the XI, not sold.** He still projects a 26.90 horizon and is the season's talisman; a 0.50 week is exactly the case the GW25 rule says to ride rather than liquidate. He stays in the starting eleven because **the autosub covers him**: if he plays zero minutes a bench player replaces him automatically, which is strictly better than pre-emptively benching a player who might return 15. **GW28 proved the mechanism** — Haaland recorded 0 minutes, the vice took the armband, and B.Fernandes' 13 doubled to 26.

**Vice: Tarkowski.** Everton are **home to Burnley (FDR1)** with no cup tie in the window and a 9–11 day gap — per this week's research the lowest rotation risk in the league. After GW28, where the vice actually fired and rescued the week, I am picking the vice for *reliability* rather than ceiling, deliberately.

**Rice benched (2.86).** He limped out of GW28 at 76' with muscle tightness and is priced **0.62** on 50.4 expected minutes; Arteta on 3 March: "I hope so. He was much better today. We haven't trained much as we only have two days to prepare." A two-day turnaround on a muscle complaint is a bench, not a start — and again, not a sale.

**The Arsenal bloc and Haaland stay LOCKED through BGW31.** The solve proposed the teardown for a fourth straight week (Gabriel → Guéhi, Rice → Semenyo, +10.34) and it is refused on the same arithmetic, plus one extra: **both proposed buys are Man City assets who blank in GW31 themselves.** The reshape does not solve the blank it is motivated by. Since GW28 we hold twelve GW31 players and can field a legal eleven; the problem is closed and the Free Hit and Wildcard both stay banked.

**Bench hair: one CALL OWED at 0.20 — Konaté sits, Calvert-Lewin starts.** Going with the solver, and against the standing lean, because this week has a specific sourced reason rather than a coin-flip: **Liverpool play Wolves twice in four days** (league Tuesday, FA Cup Friday) and this is **Slot's first fully fit squad in months** — the exact conditions for resting a centre-back, and Konaté is priced accordingly.
