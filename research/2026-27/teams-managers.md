## 0. Headline reads (the short version)

1. **A record eight-to-nine new managers**, including at Man City (Guardiola gone), Liverpool (Slot sacked), Chelsea, and Spurs. New-manager systems are the single most mispriced thing in FPL in August.
2. **Only ONE Premier League club plays European football in August**: Brighton, in the UECL play-off (20 & 27 Aug). Every other European club's first continental match is 8–17 Sep. **GW1–GW2 are effectively congestion-free league-wide.**
3. **Eleven clubs have no European football at all** — Chelsea, Newcastle, Spurs and Forest all dropped out. That is a large, structural rotation-free edge versus 2025/26.
4. **Every defender at the three promoted clubs costs £4.0m** — 26 of the 46 £4.0m defenders in the game are COV/HUL/IPS players ([FFS](https://www.fantasyfootballscout.co.uk/2026/07/23/fpl-2026-27-9-first-impressions-of-the-player-prices)). Coventry are the pick: champions by 11 points with the best attack *and* best defence in the Championship.
5. **Aston Villa's entire defence is £4.5m** (Konsa, Cash, Digne, Pau, Mings, Maatsen, Lindelöf) — a rare full-price-tier collapse and the best non-promoted enabler pool in the game. Villa are in the UCL, so it is enabler-with-a-caveat.
6. **Do not buy a promoted-club goalkeeper.** All three No.1 slots are unresolved (§4.5) — Coventry's target has not been signed, Hull are hunting two more keepers over Butland, and Ipswich's £4.0m Palmer lost the shirt in October 2025.

## 3. New manager, new system — market mispricings

Eight-to-nine new managers is a record. The market prices assets on **last season's role**, which is exactly wrong when the system changes. These are the specific bets.

### 3.1 Chelsea → back three under Xabi Alonso ([appointed 1 Jul 2026](https://www.premierleague.com/en/news/4677087/analysis-how-will-chelsea-look-under-alonso))

Alonso's Leverkusen shape was a **3-4-3 / 3-4-2-1** that built as 3-2-5. Combine that with **no European football** (Chelsea finished 10th) and you get:
- **Wing-backs are the asset class.** Reece James £5.5m (8.1%) and Gusto £5.0m (1.2%) are priced as full-backs in a back four. In a 3-4-3 the wing-back is the width. Malo Gusto at £5.0m and 1.2% ownership is the differential version of this bet.
- Palestra £5.5m arrived this summer and is an unknown — watch pre-season for whether he takes the right wing-back slot. **[UNVERIFIED]**
- **The under-priced bit is minutes, not talent.** Chelsea played Conference League football in 2025/26 and won it the season before; a Europe-free Chelsea means Palmer (£9.5m, 13.4%) and João Pedro (£7.5m, 47.2%) start close to every league game. Palmer at 13.4% ownership after an injury-hit 1,954-minute season is the clearest premium-mid value in the game if he is fit.
- Chelsea also **under-performed xG by ~8 points with the 2nd-best xG in the league**. Positive regression plus a new system plus no Europe is a rare triple.
- Fofana is suspended until 6 Sep; Quenda and Emegha are 75% doubts.

### 3.2 Liverpool → Iraola's attacking full-backs ([appointed 4 Jun 2026](https://www.liverpoolfc.com/news/liverpool-fc-appoint-andoni-iraola-new-head-coach))

Slot was sacked on 30 May after a 5th-place, 60-point season. Iraola's Bournemouth full-backs were among the most advanced in the league.
- **Frimpong £5.5m (3.0% owned) and Kerkez £5.5m (6.6%)** are the trade. Both are priced off disappointing 2025/26 minutes (1,032 and 2,251). Iraola's system is the one thing that turns them into attacking-return defenders. **This is the single biggest system-driven mispricing in the game right now.**
- Liverpool have the **best opening eight fixtures of any club (avg FDR 2.75)** and face none of last season's top ten in their first four.
- Counter-argument: Iraola's high line is clean-sheet-volatile — Bournemouth conceded 54 in 2025/26. Buy the full-backs for attacking returns, not for clean sheets.
- Salah has departed; **Szoboszlai (£7.0m, 40.9%) is expected to inherit penalties** ([FFS](https://www.fantasyfootballscout.co.uk/2026/07/23/fpl-2026-27-9-first-impressions-of-the-player-prices)). Isak fell £1.5m to £9.0m after a 694-minute season. Ekitiké is out until 31 Dec, which clears the striker slot.

### 3.3 Man City → Maresca's inverted full-backs are a *trap* ([appointed 29 Jun 2026](https://www.aljazeera.com/sports/2026/6/29/enzo-maresca-appointed-man-city-manager-to-succeed-pep-guardiola))

Guardiola left after ten years. Maresca's box midfield (3-2-2-3) inverts the full-backs into central midfield.
- **O'Reilly £6.5m at 24.8% ownership is the crowd buying a "City attacking full-back" that Maresca's system does not produce.** An inverted full-back registers defensive-contribution points but very few attacking returns, at defender prices. The market has not repriced this for the manager change.
- **Guéhi £6.0m (25.3%) is the better City defensive pick** — a genuine centre-back in the league's second-best defence, and he played 3,150 minutes last season.
- Rotation risk stays high. Maresca is expected to continue Guardiola-style squad rotation, and City are in the UCL from 8 Sep.
- City's opening is soft: BOU (H), cry(A), COV (H), mun(A).

### 3.4 Nott'm Forest → Glasner's wing-backs ([appointed 6 Jul 2026](https://www.nottinghamforest.co.uk/news/2026/july/06/forest-appoint-oliver-glasner-as-head-coach/))

Glasner left Palace for Forest. His 3-4-2-1 is *the* wing-back system — at Palace it turned Muñoz and Mitchell into premium FPL defenders.
- **Neco Williams £5.0m (12.0%) and Ola Aina £4.5m (4.6%)** are the direct beneficiaries. Aina at £4.5m is a cheap way to buy a Glasner wing-back before the market catches on; he played only 1,587 minutes last season, which is why he is cheap.
- Forest have **no European football**, so Gibbs-White (£8.0m) plays every week in a system built around a No.10 pair.
- Caveat: Forest are on their **fifth head coach in under a year**. Squad-to-system fit is unproven, and Murillo (75% doubt) and Savona (out) are already flagged.

### 3.5 Crystal Palace → Sage inherits the same shape ([appointed 15 Jun 2026](https://readcrystalpalace.com/2026/07/04/pierre-sage-3421-crystal-palace-tactical-reset/))

Pierre Sage's Lens shape was also **3-4-2-1**, so the Muñoz/Mitchell wing-back roles survive the manager change — this is a *continuity* case, not a disruption case, and the market may be discounting Palace assets for the wrong reason.
- Palace also carry the **largest negative xG delta in the league (−11)** and the **joint-best opening FDR (2.88)**.
- The offsetting risk: Palace are in the **Europa League league phase as holders**, from 16 Sep. GW1–GW5 are Europe-free; from GW6 onward, discount.
- **Mitchell £4.5m (7.4%)** — a wing-back who played 3,253 minutes and returned 135 points last season, at £4.5m. Best price-to-role in the defensive mid-tier.

### 3.6 Spurs → De Zerbi's first full season, and a rebuilt defence ([appointed 31 Mar 2026](https://www.premierleague.com/en/news/4623398/analysis-how-will-spurs-look-under-de-zerbi))

Spurs finished 17th with the worst xG of any surviving club (40.0). De Zerbi took over mid-season during a relegation fight and played pragmatically; whether the full press-baiting system returns is the open question. **[UNVERIFIED]**
- Six players joined since 1 May: Senesi £6.0m, Fernandes £6.0m, Tonali £5.5m, Van Hecke £5.0m, Robertson £4.5m, Dubravka £4.0m.
- **Dubravka at £4.0m is a goalkeeper who played 3,150 minutes last season, now the 3rd-most-owned cheap asset in the game at 24.5%.** But Spurs also have Vicario £4.5m and Kinsky £4.5m (14.8%) — **the No.1 is genuinely unresolved and the market is split three ways.** Do not commit until pre-season resolves it.
- Kulusevski, Xavi Simons and Odobert are all long-term injured.
