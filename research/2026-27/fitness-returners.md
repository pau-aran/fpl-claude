## 0. The number that drives everything: the World Cup calendar

The 2026 World Cup ran to **19 July 2026**. Spain beat Argentina 1–0 aet in the final (Ferran Torres 106'), and **England lost the semi-final 1–2 to Argentina on 15 July**, then played **France in the third-place play-off on 18 July**.
Sources: [FIFA — Spain v Argentina final](https://www.fifa.com/en/match-centre/match/17/285023/289292/400021543) · [Sky Sports — England 1-2 Argentina](https://www.skysports.com/football/news/11095/13562935/world-cup-2026-england-1-2-argentina-three-lions-suffer-semi-final-loss-after-stunning-late-turnaround-by-holders) · [CBS — bracket](https://www.cbssports.com/soccer/news/2026-fifa-world-cup-bracket-round-of-32-results-round-of-16-matchups-final/)

**Elimination dates → mandated rest → club-training return:**

| Stage reached | Last match | Reported return to club training | Days of club training before GW1 deadline |
|---|---|---|---|
| Final (ESP, ARG) | 19 Jul | ~10–12 Aug | **~9–11** |
| 3rd-place play-off (ENG, FRA) | 18 Jul | ~10–12 Aug | **~9–11** |
| Quarter-final (NOR, BEL, MAR, SUI) | 10–11 Jul | ~1–3 Aug | ~18–20 |
| Round of 16 (BRA) | 4–7 Jul | ~27–29 Jul | ~24–26 |
| Round of 32 (NED, SWE, GER) | 28 Jun–3 Jul | ~20–25 Jul (already back) | ~28–32 |

The Premier League's own pre-season piece states players reaching the QF/SF/final "may not report until early August, receiving extended rest before the season begins August 21" ([premierleague.com](https://www.premierleague.com/en/news/4678380/premier-league-clubs-return-for-pre-season-key-dates-friendlies-and-training-updates)). FPL Dashboard puts the mandated recovery period as ending **10–12 August** ([fpl.page](https://fpl.page/article/fpl-gw1-absent-injured-missing-players-world-cup-2627)).

**Implication:** roughly 40 FPL-relevant players will have **zero pre-season friendlies and ~10 days of training** before GW1. None of them carry an FPL flag. This is the largest, cleanest mispricing available and it is entirely invisible in the FPL UI.

**The tell nobody will act on in time:** the **Community Shield, Arsenal v Man City, 16 August** — five days before the deadline. It is the only competitive read on which late returners actually start. Most of the market drafts before it.
