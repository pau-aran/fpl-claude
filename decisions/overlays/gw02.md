# GW2 overlay — deliberately empty

`gw02.json` is `{}`. That is a decision, not an omission.

**Do not reuse `gw01.json` for GW2.** Its 56 World Cup fatigue entries were written
`duration_gws: 3`, so they would still apply to GW2 and GW3 if the file were carried forward.
They are **retired**. GW1 falsified the mechanism they encode: every named returner started
(Guéhi 90' and scored, O'Reilly 79', E. Anderson 84', Saka scored, Rice, White assisted),
and three of the players those overlays faded returned points.

The underlying read survives in corrected form and is applied in prose, not in the minutes
model: **post-tournament fatigue in 2026/27 shows up as finishing/sharpness, not as minutes.**
Maresca called the returners' state "an extension of pre-season, with a little rust"; Haaland
took 0.85 xG — a third of City's total — and scored none. That is a reason to be careful
about *captaining* a rusty returner, not a reason to reduce his projected minutes. There is
no defensible numeric share to write for it, so nothing is written.

The non-fatigue entries in `gw01.json` (the Dubravka club-change correction, the Lacroix
transfer ban, the Ipswich blind-spot calls) were squad-selection judgements for the opening
build and have no GW2 successor: the squad is already picked and none of those players is in
it or being bought.

Rebuild an overlay for GW3 from scratch, against real team news, on a machine that can reach
the FPL API. See `reports/reviews/2026-27/gw01.md` §2.1 and `decisions/gw02.md` §2.1.
