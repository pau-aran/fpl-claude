## 1. Thiaw → De Cuyper — declined, on a corrected fixture read

The GW2 memo queued this move on a **two-gameweek** comparison: Brighton's GW3-4 (LEE H 2,
COV A 2 = 4) against Newcastle's GW3-4 (BOU H 3, LEE A 3 = 6). That comparison was carried
forward into this memo's first draft without re-checking it against a fuller horizon. It
doesn't survive one:

| GW | Newcastle (Thiaw) | Brighton (De Cuyper) |
|---|---|---|
| 3 | BOU (H) FDR3 | LEE (H) FDR2 |
| 4 | LEE (A) FDR3 | COV (A) FDR2 |
| 5 | **HUL (H) FDR2** | **ARS (H) FDR4** |
| 6 | **COV (A) FDR2** | SUN (A) FDR3 |
| 7 | AVL (H) FDR3 | CRY (H) FDR3 |
| 8 | CRY (A) FDR3 | LIV (A) FDR4 |
| **GW3-4 sum** | 6 | **4** |
| **GW5-6 sum** | **4** | 7 |
| **GW3-8 sum** | **16** | 18 |
| **Sum FDR (1-8), committed grid** | **23** | 25 |

**Brighton's run is only better for exactly the two gameweeks the original comparison used.**
From GW5 it inverts hard: Newcastle get a genuinely good back-to-back (Hull home, Coventry
away, both FDR2) while Brighton walk into Arsenal at home and never drop back below FDR3
through GW8. Over the fuller GW3-8 window — the horizon this squad is actually held for, not
just the next transfer window — Newcastle's run is the easier one, and the season-long grid
agrees (23 vs 25).

**Three more reasons this reinforces rather than just neutralises the case for holding:**

1. **Thiaw delivered exactly what he was bought for in GW2** — a clean sheet away at
   Tottenham, no flag, clearly still first-choice. Selling a nailed, in-form defender into a
   run that (properly checked) is better than the alternative's is the wrong direction on
   both the "who's playing well" and "whose fixtures are better" axes at once.
2. **De Cuyper's own evidence so far is fixture-sensitive, which undercuts the positional-
   arbitrage case for ignoring FDR on him.** GW1 (Villa at home, FDR3): 17 points. GW2
   (Chelsea away, FDR4): no return (GW2 review §2.1). Two matches is not proof, but it points
   the same direction as the fixture grid, not against it — there's no standing evidence yet
   that his advanced role makes him fixture-immune the way, say, a penalty-taking midfielder
   might be.
3. **This removes a real, already-flagged risk for free.** The original draft's own §1 named
   De Cuyper's unverified live price as "the binding constraint on this week's only move."
   Not making the move retires that risk entirely rather than hoping Plan B doesn't trigger.

**Held. No transfer.** De Cuyper moves from "queued" to a plain watch-list name — worth
re-pricing the comparison again once Brighton's run turns back in their favour (their fixture
list beyond GW8 isn't in the committed grid; check it when it's built).

## 5. Risks, stated plainly

1. **The original draft of this memo recommended a transfer built on a two-gameweek fixture
   window that didn't hold up against the fuller run.** Corrected before entry, but it's a
   real process point: a "the run turns good" case needs to be checked past the immediate
   window it's cited for, every time, not just when challenged. Written up as a lesson for
   next week's review.
2. **The Mbeumo deferral remains a bet that this week's evidence (his goal, Bruno's
   hat-trick) is noise and the fixture-difficulty thesis still holds.** If Man Utd's front
   three keeps producing against EVE(A)/MCI(H)/FUL(A), this costs real points and should be
   logged as a genuine miss, not excused as process.
3. **Arsenal's hard fixture (CHE H, FDR4) is held through, not reacted to** — consistent with
   `gw02.md`'s read that the Arsenal question is GW4-6 (their run softens at GW6, LEE H,
   FDR2), not a one-bad-week reaction.
4. **DefCon points remain completely unmodelled** (GW2 review §1) for four owned defenders
   including Thiaw, now held rather than sold. Carried forward as a standing model gap.
5. **The pipeline has not run live for three consecutive gameweeks.** Every number in this
   memo, including the corrected fixture table in §1, is hand-derived from the committed FDR
   grid — correct as far as it goes, but a real solve would price this properly instead of by
   FDR sum, which is a blunt proxy for fixture difficulty at best.
6. **An unverified "Isak to Liverpool" transfer surfaced in this week's sweep**, alongside
   other headlines flagged as high-risk for being stale/misdated (GW2 review §4). Not acted
   on; relevant to Wirtz's service if true, worth a clean re-check next week.
7. **De Cuyper and Kerkez/Frimpong all remain watch-list names**, not owned or being bought
   this week — re-open both at GW4 with fresher evidence rather than carrying either forward
   on this week's numbers.
